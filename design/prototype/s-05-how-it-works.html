How it works

Ordering is easy.

Three steps and one message.

  1. 1

    Message us

    Send “HI” on WhatsApp or DM @tiffinbox.lhr on Instagram.

  2. 2

    Pick a plan and pay

    Choose weekly or monthly and pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer.

  3. 3

    Eat on time

    Your first meal arrives the next day: lunch by 1 PM, dinner by 8 PM.

The weekly menu is shared every Sunday evening on WhatsApp, Instagram and the website.

Delivery times and skipping

Delivery time and skip cut-off for each meal

Times come from admin settings (A-08).

Skip a meal

Up to 3 skips a week. Skipped meals are added to the end of your plan. Tell us on WhatsApp before the cut-off.

Pause your plan

Going home or on leave? Pause the plan on WhatsApp and restart when you’re back.

Packaging

Meals come sealed in tiffin boxes so they stay hot and clean.

Payment

You pay in advance, weekly or monthly.

  • Methods: JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer.
  • Weekly plans: paid before the week starts (Sunday).
  • Monthly plans: paid before the month starts; 5% cheaper than weekly.
  • Offices: one monthly invoice for the whole team.
  • Receipts: sent on WhatsApp after every payment.

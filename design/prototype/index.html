Stage 1 design prototype. Static HTML, synthetic data, placeholders for logo, photos and unknown contact details. Not production code.

Tiffin Box: clickable prototype

Every screen from the PRD inventory (section 7). Open each at phone and desktop width. State variants sit at the bottom of each page under “States on this screen”.

Placeholders: logo, food photos, [WhatsApp number], [order hours], [website] have not been received. Fonts load from Google Fonts in this prototype only. WhatsApp buttons open a note instead of WhatsApp.
